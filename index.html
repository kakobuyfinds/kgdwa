How to use the spreadsheet

  1. Find the seller listing

    Open a HipobuyFind product page and locate its original marketplace link. If you plan to use LoveGoBuy, check whether it accepts that link and can order the size and color you want.

  2. Check fit and QC photos

    Compare the seller’s size chart with a garment you own. If warehouse quality-control photos are available, check the tag, color, stitching and measurements against your order.

  3. Compare shipping quotes

    Check the parcel’s actual and volumetric weight, available routes and any item restrictions. Review the seller and agent’s return terms before approving dispatch.

Estimate your landed cost. Add the selected variant price, domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Convert all amounts to the same currency before comparing totals.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

This is an independent browsing guide that uses HipobuyFind listings. Confirm product compatibility, ordering terms and shipping options directly with your chosen agent.

What does the HipobuyFind item number identify?

It identifies a product page in the HipobuyFind catalog. When placing an order, use the original seller link and specify the exact variant.

Can I open the catalog in Google Sheets or Excel?

Yes. Filter the catalog and download the visible results as CSV. The file includes product titles, item numbers, categories, CNY reference prices and source links.

What if the seller link is unavailable?

Search HipobuyFind for the product name or a similar style, then open the new seller listing and verify the photos, measurements and price for your variant before ordering.
